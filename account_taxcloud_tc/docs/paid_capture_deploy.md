# Paid invoice TaxCloud notifications

Upgrade `account_taxcloud_tc` to 1.0.11 after the parent repository points to the matching submodule commit. The module creates two technical invoice fields, extends the customer invoice search, and creates **TaxCloud: Send Paid Invoice Captured Notifications**. The scheduled action is active by default and runs every three minutes as the installation user. Confirm the scheduled action, its owner, and its next execution after upgrade. The existing **TaxCloud: Send Deferred Invoice Captures** job continues to send `AuthorizedWithCapture`.

New TaxCloud sales invoices queue their `Captured` notification when Odoo records the payment or transitions into payment. The paid-notification job waits for any pending `AuthorizedWithCapture` report. It claims invoices using row locks, records each claim before calling TaxCloud, and processes up to 25 invoices with a 50-second start budget. The SOAP operation times out after 15 seconds. A setup error before the SOAP request retries after 15 minutes. Invoice posting, tax validation, and refund handling keep their current paths.

In Accounting → Customers → Invoices, use **TaxCloud Payment Notification Review** to find provider errors and interrupted sends. Compare the invoice's Odoo record ID (the TaxCloud order ID) with the provider record. An interrupted run or unsuccessful SOAP response may leave the provider outcome uncertain. Odoo holds these records for review and never resubmits them automatically. This upgrade queues future payment transitions and includes no historical replay.
